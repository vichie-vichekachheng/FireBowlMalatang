ABOUT FIREBOWL

Our Story & Passion

Discover what makes FireBowl Malatang the ultimate destination for authentic, customizable Chinese hot pot in Phnom Penh.

Our Journey

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted with Passion

Founded with a love for authentic Chinese hot pot culture, FireBowl Malatang started with a simple vision: to bring the bold, comforting, and fiery flavors of traditional street-style malatang to Phnom Penh.

We believe that a great meal is personal. That’s why we handpick our ingredients daily and let you take control of your bowl—from selecting your favorite noodles and crisp vegetables to choosing your exact spice level.

Why Choose FireBowl?

What makes our malatang experience unforgettable

Farm-Fresh Quality

We source our vegetables, meats, and seafood daily to ensure crisp freshness and rich flavor in every single bite.

Secret Spice Blends

Our signature broths are slow-simmered for hours using authentic Sichuan spices, herbs, and chili peppers.

100% Customizable

You are the chef. Pick your ingredients by weight, choose your broth, and enjoy a bowl made strictly to your taste.

Ready to Feel the Heat?

Visit our location today or order online to enjoy a steaming, flavorful bowl of malatang.
